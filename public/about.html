I'm an AI engineer specializing in the design and deployment of intelligent systems — with a focus on making LLMs reliable, observable, and useful in real-world applications.

My work centres on three connected areas: agentic AI (autonomous agents that reason and act over time), retrieval-augmented generation (grounding LLM responses in factual, up-to-date information), and multi-agent coordination (systems where multiple AI agents collaborate to complete complex tasks).

Beyond the AI layer, I apply these capabilities to domain problems — most recently financial education, where AI can make complex concepts genuinely accessible.

I build in the open. Every project is an experiment in what it takes to move a research idea to production-grade code.
